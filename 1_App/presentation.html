4 • Key Learning #1

Start From the Tech Blog — "Every Prompt Took 1 Hour to Understand"

📖 1 Hr / Prompt

🔗 The Starting Point

https://aistudio.google.com/learn/building-3d-games-and-worlds-with-gemini-4-argon

  • Don't Blindly Copy-Paste: Spent 1 full hour per prompt understanding the 3D architecture first.
  • What Matter Most: Scene graph, `InstancedMesh` batching, 0 allocations in `requestAnimationFrame`, and `< 350 lines` per file.
/goal Build Stage 1 only: Bay South 3D peninsula + water + paths. STRICT RULES: 1. Max 350 lines per file under src/world/ 2. Zero allocations inside requestAnimationFrame 3. Spawn QA Critic subagent to verify 60 FPS & 0 errors

4 • Key Learning #2

Job Shift: Tell AI What to Do, QA It, and Guard the Product "Why"

🧭 AI Director

🔄 Our Job Now

  • Used to be: Dev / PM figuring out how to build.
  • Now is:
    1️⃣ Tell AI what to do
    2️⃣ QA whether & where it is actually done.

⚠️ The New Danger

  • Because AI builds so fast, it is much harder to understand the Product WHY and Product VALUE (WHAT).
  • When execution is free, product taste & QA are everything.
🛠️ Built `admin.html` to QA Lighting & Slow-Mo Rigs🔍
💡 Product Why: Cut 65w Essay → 12w Aha! Toybox🔍
🗣️ Note: Look at the bottom right: AI happily wrote 65-word encyclopedia essays, which nobody reads in an arcade game! Human product sense cut it to 12 words + a 3D toybox.

4 • Key Learning #4

Two Power Prompts That Changed Everything

⚡ Copy & Use

🔥 Prompt 1: Subagent Roundtable to 9/10

Spin into different subagents (Game Designer, 3D Artist, Perf Engineer, QA Critic) and have a roundtable discussion. Fix every gap until everyone scores it 9/10 or above.

🎭 Prompt 2: Prompt the Emotion

This is the Emotion I wanna achieve: 1. WOW! (Living sky & Night Rhapsody) 2. AMAZED (12-word Aha! relics) 3. FUN (Spicy bot rivalry) 4. FUNNY (Durian slip traps)
🍌 "FUNNY" Output: Spiky Durian Traps & Tram Knockback🔍
🔥 "9/10 Roundtable" Output: 4-Player Chase Action🔍
🗣️ Note: Asking for a "9/10 subagent roundtable" and stating "This is the Emotion I want to achieve" pushed the game from a tech demo to a fun product.
Learning #4: 9/10 Subagent Consensus + Emotion-Driven Prompting Slide 8 / 10

Wrap-Up & Q&A

6 Key Takeaways from Building in 3D with AI

🙌 Q&A Time

1️⃣ 1 Hr / Prompt

Start from the Gemini 4 3D blog. Understand the prompt deeply before running.

2️⃣ Guard the "Why"

We tell AI what & QA where. Protecting Product Why & Value is our real job.

3️⃣ Image Milestones

Pinterest + ChatGPT/Imagen 2 mockups (`Screens 01–11`) beat long text specs.

4️⃣ Two Power Prompts

"Subagent roundtable to 9/10" + "This is the Emotion I want to achieve".

5️⃣ Route by Cost

Gemini 4 for complex 3D/architecture; Gemini 2.5 for simple fixes.

6️⃣ Keep Screenshots

Save visual records (`progress.html` + `v1–v5.zip`) so adjustments are effortless.
