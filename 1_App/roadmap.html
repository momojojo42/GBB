POST-MVP ROADMAP • TEXT SPECIFICATION ONLY

Next 5 Milestones After MVP Go-Live

With the Gardens by the Bay 3D Treasure Hunt MVP live on the Game Page and debug tools isolated in the Admin View, the following five post-MVP milestones define the upcoming expansion roadmap. Per project scope, this page documents the product and engineering specifications in text form only.

MILESTONE #1

Multi-Player Mode — Same-Room Real-Time Competition

✅ Shipped • v3.2 Live

Allow multiple human players to join the same shared room and compete head-to-head in real time across the 3D garden peninsula (shipped in v3.2 with 3-step Party Room creation, 3D Supertree Grove Waiting Lobby, and synchronized 3 • 2 • 1 • GO! starting-line countdown).

Player Experience & Room Flow (Shipped v3.2)

  • Shared Room Lobbies (Step 1): Players create a private Party Room with a 6-character invite code (GBB-XXX), copy a shareable invite link (?room=GBB-XXX), or join a friend's room code directly from the Frosted-Pearl Start Card.
  • 3D Supertree Grove Waiting Lobby & Hybrid AI Backfill (Step 2): All 4 Chibi Explorers line up on glowing color-coded platform rings in Supertree Grove Plaza with a live 4-slot roster, 🤖 Hybrid AI Bot Backfill toggle switch, and 3D lobby quick-emotes (👋 Wave, 🤚 Chope!, 🎉 Hype!).
  • Synchronized 3-2-1-GO Countdown & Live 3D Presence (Step 3): Authoritative 3 • 2 • 1 • GO! starting-line countdown releases all racers simultaneously at 03:00 with live floating nameplates and atomic relic snatching.

Technical & Sync Specification (Shipped v3.2)

  • Authoritative Room Server & Dual Transport: Zero-dependency RFC 6455 WebSocket upgrade server (/ws-multiplayer) paired with automatic BroadcastChannel('gbb-multiplayer-v32') fallback, maintaining authoritative match state and atomic 42-relic pickup locks (CLAIM_RELIC → RELIC_LOCKED / RELIC_CHOPED).
  • 20 Hz Transform Interpolation: 20 Hz client position/yaw/velocity snapshots smoothed via zero-GC 60 FPS Hermite dead-reckoning interpolation (RemoteTransformInterpolator.js).
  • Strict Modular Codebase (< 400 Lines Gate): All .js and .css modules refactored and audited to remain strictly under 400 lines per file.
MILESTONE #2

Mobile View — Touch Controls & Mobile Viewport Optimization

Planned • Post-MVP Phase 2

Optimize the 3D gameplay experience, camera ergonomics, and HUD layout specifically for mobile phones and tablets in both landscape and portrait orientations.

Touch Ergonomics & Responsive HUD

  • Dual-Thumb Touch Controls: Left-thumb virtual analog joystick for 360° movement + right-thumb swipe-to-orbit camera and dedicated thumb-arc action buttons (Collect, Jump, Sonar, Field Gadgets).
  • Compact Collapsible HUD: Condense top/bottom HUD cards into sleek mobile drawers and safe-area-aware status pills so over 80% of the mobile viewport remains unobstructed 3D world space.
  • Touch Target Sizing: Enforce a minimum 48×48 CSS pixel tap target across all interactive mobile controls.

Mobile WebGL Performance Budget

  • Adaptive Resolution Scaling: Dynamic devicePixelRatio clamping (1.0–1.5x on mobile GPUs) to sustain 60 FPS without thermal throttling.
  • Mobile Shader & Post-FX Tier: Automatic mobile graphics profile with half-resolution UnrealBloom pass, 1024px shadow maps, and aggressive frustum/distance culling.
MILESTONE #3

New Maps — Singapore Botanic Gardens (UNESCO) Map, MBS & Sentosa

✅ Map 02 Shipped • v3.3 Live

Expand beyond Gardens by the Bay with new playable 3D Singapore landmark maps, starting with Singapore Botanic Gardens (1859 UNESCO World Heritage Site — shipped in v3.3!), followed by Marina Bay Sands (MBS) and Sentosa Island.

New Playable 3D Map Worlds

  • Map 02 — Singapore Botanic Gardens (UNESCO World Heritage — Shipped v3.3): Features The Bandstand (1930) octagonal gazebo, 18 Giant Heritage Tembusu & Rain Trees (including the iconic $5-Note Tembusu Tree & SPH Walk of Giants canopy boardwalk), National Orchid Garden (Burkill Hall, Sembcorp Cool House & Tan Hoon Siang Mist House), Swan Lake & Ginger Waterfall cavern, Shaw Foundation Symphony Stage, and Jacob Ballas Children's Garden with 42 UNESCO & botanical relics.
  • Map 03 — Marina Bay Sands (MBS) & Sentosa Island: Upcoming expansions featuring the triple hotel towers, ArtScience Museum, Helix Bridge, and Sentosa Siloso/Palawan coastal trails.

Modular World Loader Architecture (Shipped v3.3)

  • Multi-Map World Registry (`MapRegistry.js` & `MapWorldSwitcherController.js`): Dynamic in-place world switching between Map 01 (`?map=gbb`) and Map 02 (`?map=sbg`) via the Start Screen pill bar and Top Global Bar switcher.
  • Calibrated Cartography & Physics Parity: Full 5-zone layout, 42 relics, 6 enterable landmark footprints, 5 bridges, and 2D metric SVG floorplan (`public/maps/sbg_floorplan_2d_meters.svg`).
MILESTONE #4

Authentication — Player Accounts, Persistent Scores & Admin Lock

Planned • Post-MVP Phase 4

Add user authentication so players can sign in, persistently record their match scores and 42-relic collection progress, compete on global leaderboards, and lock down the Admin View.

Player Identity & Persistent Leaderboards

  • Frictionless Sign-In: Support OAuth social login (Google / Apple) and passwordless magic-link email sign-in, plus instant guest-to-account upgrade after finishing a match.
  • Cloud Score & Passport Sync: Record fastest 42/42 Relaxed completion times, highest 4-Player Timed Race scores, combo streaks, and lifetime Botanical Passport discoveries per map.
  • Global & Friends Leaderboards: Filterable daily, weekly, and all-time leaderboards across Gardens by the Bay, MBS, and Sentosa maps.

Security & Admin View Access Control

  • Role-Based Admin Lock (/admin.html): Wire the existing Admin Auth Guard to verify server-issued JWT claims (role: "admin"), locking the Screen Switcher and LookDev Studio strictly to authorized developers.
  • Server-Verified Score Submissions: Validate match duration and relic pickup timestamps server-side before recording leaderboard entries.
MILESTONE #5

Improved Avatars — Distinct Explorer Classes & Unique Special Powers

Planned • Post-MVP Phase 5

Upgrade the Chibi Explorer roster so players can choose from distinct 3D avatars, each equipped with a unique passive trait and an active signature special power.

Explorer Classes & Signature Powers

  • Aero-Sprinter Avatar (Velocity Specialist): Equipped with solar-jet boots that grant a higher base dash speed and a Canopy Wind-Glide power to leap across water channels.
  • Botanical Naturalist Avatar (Resonance Specialist): Carries a bioluminescent orchid staff whose Flora Bloom Pulse reveals and magnetizes all relics within an expanded 18m radius.
  • Chrono-Pathfinder Avatar (Tactical Specialist): Wears holographic visor goggles with a Waypoint Phase Blink ability and longer combo-streak decay timers.
  • Mirage Trickster Avatar (Disruption Specialist): Deploys twin Holo-Clone Decoys that distract rival bots/players while granting brief stealth sprint speed.

Balance & Customization Design

  • Cooldown & Energy Balance: Signature powers charge via relic pickups and combo streaks, ensuring fair competitive balance in both solo AI races and Milestone #1 multiplayer rooms.
  • Visual Distinction in 3D: Unique Blender `.glb` headgear, backpack silhouettes, and color-coded particle trails so every avatar's active power is immediately readable in 3D.
